StyleSync Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

1. Eligibility

You may request a refund for the StyleSync Pro upgrade within 14 calendar days of purchase, provided that:

  • You have not activated the Pro license on more than one machine.
  • The product has been downloaded and used for fewer than 7 days total.

2. How to request a refund

Email support@lxstudio.dev with:

  • Your order number (from the Paddle receipt).
  • A brief explanation of why you are requesting the refund.

Refunds are processed within 5 business days upon approval.

3. Non-refundable cases

Refunds will not be issued if:

  • The request is made after 14 days from purchase.
  • You have used the Pro license on multiple machines.
  • The product was obtained through unauthorized resellers.

4. Free tier

No refund applies to the free tier, as no payment is collected.

5. Digital content exception (EU 2011/83/EU Art. 11)

By activating the Pro license, you acknowledge that you consent to immediate performance of the digital service and waive your right of withdrawal under Article 11 of EU Directive 2011/83/EU. You confirm that you have read and accepted this waiver before completing the purchase.

6. Changes

We may update this policy at any time. The version in effect at the time of purchase governs your transaction.
